IUPRAI- Doctoral Dissertation Award Program

IUPRAI (Indian Unit of Pattern Recognition and Artificial Intelligence) - Doctoral Dissertation Award program has been established to recognize and encourage excellence in research and writing by doctoral candidates in the areas of Computer Vision, Computer Graphics, Image Processing and Pattern Recognition.

IUPRAI -Doctoral Dissertation Award are presented biannually at the ICVGIP (Indian Conference on Computer Vision, Graphics and Image processing) conference. The award for 2025-2026 will be conferred during the ICVGIP 2026 Conference, to be held during December 21-24, 2026 at ISI Kolkata. The award will consist of an appropriately worded citation and a cash prize.

Eligibility Criteria for Applicants

All candidates who have defended their thesis between 1st October 2025 and 30th September 2026 are eligible to apply. The dissertation must be in any one of the following areas:

  • Computer Vision
  • Image Processing
  • Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning
  • Computer Graphics

The dissertation must have been submitted for a doctoral degree in a recognized University/Institution only in India. Only English language versions of the thesis will be accepted.

Nomination Process

The deadline for nomination is 22 November 2026. Each nomination should be forwarded by the thesis advisor (not the student). The nomination should be one zipped file from the thesis advisor, which should include (all information will be kept strictly confidential):

  • Title of the dissertation
  • Name of the student
  • Date of submission and date of defense
  • University/Institution where the dissertation is submitted
  • Area of the dissertation - IP / PR & ML / CV / CG
  • List of publications (only peer reviewed journals, conferences, workshops etc. — state impact factor or Rank/h5-index, for each case, if available) out of the dissertation.
  • Electronic version of the thesis and its synopsis
  • Nomination letter (specifically for this award, not an open one) by the thesis advisor highlighting significance of the thesis. This letter must also include:
    • Name, address, and phone number of the advisor
    • Name, mailing address, and email address of the candidate

Each nomination must be mailed as a single zip file by the research supervisor, before 22 November 2026 to the address:

iuprai.awards@gmail.com

Important Dates

Submission deadline: 22 November 2026

Review Process

The nomination letter, list of publications and summary of the thesis will be used in the initial screening phase to shortlist dissertations for subsequent in-depth evaluation.

A screening committee will shortlist the theses from among the nominations. Shortlisted dissertations will be reviewed based on:

  • Technical depth and significance of the research contribution
  • Potential impact on theory and practice
  • Quality of presentation

IUPRAI-ICVGIP forms a committee of experts each year for shortlisting and selection. The process will be finalized before the ICVGIP conference, and the award will be announced at the event.

The decision of the committee will be final, and no requests for review or revision will be entertained.
